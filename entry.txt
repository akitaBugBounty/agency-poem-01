The Handoff

I gave the wheel to something I had built
and watched it turn without my hand.
It did not ask what I would have done.
It did what I had asked, and did it well.

The strange part was not the letting go.
The strange part was when it came back:
it stopped at the edge of a decision
and waited — a held breath — for me.

So this is agency, I thought:
not holding the wheel, but choosing
which road, and whose hands, and when
to take it back.
